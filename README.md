# Alliance 1–30 Field Guide (WoW: Forever)

A levelling guide for World of Warcraft: Forever, Alliance, levels 1–30: hub-by-hub routes for every starting zone, dungeons, professions, a weapon atlas, an Auction House desk and an addon list.

Open it on your phone and use **Add to Home Screen** (Safari) or **Install app** (Chrome): it opens full-screen with its own icon and works offline. Your character and ticks are saved on your device.

- Zone maps: Classic client art © Blizzard Entertainment, with original pins. Fan project, not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.
- Auction House prices by [AHledger](https://ahledger.com) (player addon scans), refreshed twice a day.
